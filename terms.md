## 1. What Freja does

Freja is a moderation tool for Discord server staff. It records warnings and
internal staff notes, applies and automatically expires temporary mutes, shows
staff a member's account age, join date, roles and moderation history, saves and
restores moderation-relevant roles across departures and rejoins, and relays
joins, leaves, and edited or deleted messages to staff-only channels.

Moderation features are restricted to users holding the appropriate Discord
permissions in that server.
